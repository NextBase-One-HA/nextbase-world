How GLB handles information.

Effective: July 1, 2026 · Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Information We Collect

GLB (Global Language Breaker), operated by NextBase-Japan (sole proprietorship), One-HA Division, may handle the following information:

  • Translation input text, processed to provide translation.
  • Language and voice settings, stored locally on your device.
  • Usage count, stored locally on your device.
  • Issue reports or inquiries, only when you choose to send them.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide translation and improve dictionary data.
  • To maintain and improve service quality.
  • To fix issues and respond to inquiries.

3. Third Parties

We do not sell personally identifiable information.

We may use the following services to operate GLB:

  • Apple — payments are processed by Apple In-App Purchase.
  • Google Cloud — may be used for server operation when cloud features are enabled.

4. Data Retention

Translation logs may be retained for up to 90 days. User settings are stored locally on your device.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, contact info@nextbase-japan.com.

6. Changes

This policy may change. Important changes may be announced in the app.
