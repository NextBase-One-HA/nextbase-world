Service and pricing

Service
A productivity utility that photographs or imports a schedule, prepares calendar candidates on the iPhone, and writes to Calendar only after one human batch confirmation.
Free function
3 successful schedule imports per calendar month. One confirmed Calendar save counts as one import even if several events are added together.
Premium function
Unlimited confirmed Calendar adds while an active Monthly Unlimited entitlement is verified.
Premium price
The exact localized price displayed by Apple governs. The reference price is US$3.99 per month where applicable.
Product identifier
com.nextbase.schedulesnap.monthly
Payment method
In-app purchase through Apple and the user’s Apple Account.
Delivery
Eligible Premium functions become available after Apple confirms the entitlement. No physical product is shipped.

Renewal, cancellation, and refunds

Monthly Unlimited is an auto-renewable monthly subscription. It renews automatically unless cancelled in Apple Account Settings before the end of the current billing period. Refund requests and billing disputes are handled by Apple. NEXTBASE does not receive full payment-card information.

On-device processing

Reading of schedule photos is designed to occur on the iPhone. Event Sniper does not send schedule photos to an external AI API. Apple StoreKit is used for purchases.
