1. Core Privacy Design

AI GUARD & BRAKE is designed to perform its core GUARD / BRAKE functionality using capabilities available on the user’s device.

NextBase does not need to know the content of your conversations with AI services in order to provide the core GUARD / BRAKE function.

2. AI Conversation Content

AI GUARD & BRAKE does not intentionally collect, read, upload, or analyze the contents of your conversations with third-party AI apps as part of its core GUARD / BRAKE functionality.

3. App Selection and Identifiers

The device may process information required to identify and restrict apps selected by the user.

Where Apple system frameworks provide privacy-preserving identifiers or representations, AI GUARD & BRAKE relies on those platform mechanisms.

These identifiers or representations are used for app functionality. This app functionality does not require NextBase to collect your name, postal address, or telephone number.

4. Version-Specific Data Handling

Current App Store version 1.1.1

iPhone BRAKE does not require a NextBase account. CLOUD BRAKE is optional. Connecting CLOUD BRAKE uses Sign in with Apple. NextBase does not receive your Apple ID password.

When CLOUD BRAKE is connected, NextBase stores a SHA-256 hash of the Apple subject rather than the raw subject, an internal User ID derived from that hash, a server-issued Device ID, the device public key, the Apple refresh token needed to manage or revoke the connection, and service state and timestamps needed to provide the CLOUD BRAKE connection.

The one-time Sign in with Apple authorization code is used for the token exchange and is not persisted. NextBase does not store the device private key, your Apple ID password, or the contents of your AI conversations.

My Page can delete that account and the CLOUD BRAKE records NextBase holds for it. Disconnecting a device stops that device connection and does not by itself delete the account.

1.1.2 candidate

The 1.1.2 candidate does not expose the CLOUD BRAKE / Sign in with Apple connection path described above. Its public product scope is selecting supported apps, applying GUARD / BRAKE on this iPhone, and restoring access through user-controlled UNLOCK.

For that app functionality, the app may use identifiers or privacy-preserving representations supplied by Apple or iOS to identify selected apps and enforce restrictions. NextBase does not require your name, postal address, or telephone number for this functionality.

While 1.1.1 remains the version publicly distributed on the App Store, App Store privacy information remains aligned to 1.1.1. Any later change to a “Data Not Collected” disclosure will only be considered after 1.1.2 is publicly released and the released behavior is freshly verified.
