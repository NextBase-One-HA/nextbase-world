Privacy Policy

Core BRAKE does not need the content of your AI conversations.

Effective Date: September 21, 2026 · NextBase

1. Core Privacy Design

AI BRAKE is designed to perform its core BRAKE functionality using capabilities available on the user’s device.

NextBase does not need to know the content of your conversations with AI services in order to provide the core BRAKE function.

2. AI Conversation Content

AI BRAKE does not intentionally collect, read, upload, or analyze the contents of your conversations with third-party AI apps as part of its core BRAKE functionality.

3. App Selection

The device may process information required to identify and restrict apps selected by the user.

Where Apple system frameworks provide privacy-preserving identifiers or representations, AI BRAKE relies on those platform mechanisms.

4. Personal Information

AI BRAKE should not require creation of a NextBase account for its core BRAKE functionality unless a future feature explicitly states otherwise.

If a future version introduces optional services that collect personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated before that collection begins.

5. Third-Party AI Services

AI BRAKE does not control the privacy practices of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any other third-party service.

Your interaction with those services is governed by the privacy policies of their respective providers.

6. Apple Services

AI BRAKE relies on certain Apple platform capabilities.

Information handled directly by Apple or iOS is governed by Apple’s applicable privacy terms and system behavior.

7. Analytics and Advertising

AI BRAKE will not claim that analytics or advertising data is absent unless the shipped build has been verified accordingly.

Any analytics, diagnostics, advertising, or tracking introduced in a future version must be disclosed here and in App Store privacy information before release.

8. Tracking

AI BRAKE does not use user activity for cross-company tracking unless explicitly disclosed and handled in accordance with Apple’s App Tracking Transparency requirements.

9. Children

AI BRAKE is not specifically designed as a child-directed service.

Users and guardians are responsible for determining whether use is appropriate under applicable local laws and family settings.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the information actually handled by AI BRAKE.

No software or device environment can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. International Availability

Privacy rights vary by jurisdiction.

Where applicable law grants rights regarding personal data, NextBase will honor those rights as legally required for data actually held by NextBase.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s data practices or legal obligations change.
