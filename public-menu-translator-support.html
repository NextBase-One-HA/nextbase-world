How to use

On the top screen, tap Photograph a menu. Allow Camera if iOS asks, then point at a menu, sign, label, or readable text. You can also pick a photo from the album on the camera screen. Live translation can appear automatically. Use the shutter if you want to freeze that scene, then Save or Share.

No-text scenes show “No text detected.” Labels, signs, notices, and general text are in scope. A price is not required except as a menu signal.

What you should see on a result

  • Translation
  • Original text
  • Price and home-currency estimate when OCR evidence includes them
  • AI explanation when on-device intelligence is available
  • Save / Share after a captured (frozen) scene

If Apple Intelligence is available, the card may explain the visible text. If not, you still get OCR / translation / price.

Camera permission

If the live view does not appear, enable Camera in iOS Settings for Menu Translator, then return to the app. Saving a captured scene also needs Photo Library add permission.
