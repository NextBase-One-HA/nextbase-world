Event Sniper Support

Photograph a schedule, review the candidates, and add only what you confirm. Reading stays on this iPhone.

Support language: English · Typical response time: 3–5 business days

What Event Sniper does

Event Sniper reads a paper schedule, whiteboard, or screenshot on the iPhone and prepares calendar candidates. You review them and add the selected items together. Calendar is not written until you confirm.

Contact

Email info@nextbase-japan.com. Include the app version, iOS version, iPhone model, and a short description of the expected result and actual result. Do not send private unedited photos of other people’s schedules, identity documents, or confidential material.

Basic flow

  1. Allow Camera access.
  2. Photograph a clear printed timetable, or choose a photo.
  3. Wait for candidates.
  4. Deselect anything you do not want. Edit a row if a date or time is incomplete.
  5. Tap the batch add button once.
  6. Allow Calendar write access when asked.
  7. Share only if you want to, using iOS Share Sheet.

Ambiguous dates

Values such as 03/04 are not guessed. Select the row and choose the date before adding.

Language

The interface follows the iPhone language when a translation is included. Fallback is English. Current interface languages include English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Hindi, Arabic, and Turkish.

Free and Premium

Free: 3 successful schedule imports per calendar month. Premium: unlimited confirmed Calendar adds while the subscription is active. Restore Purchases is in Settings and on the paywall.

Camera or Calendar permission denied

Open iOS Settings → Event Sniper and enable Camera and Calendars. Existing events are not read. Write access is used only to add events you confirm.
