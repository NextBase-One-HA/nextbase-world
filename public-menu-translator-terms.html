2. What the app does

Point the camera at a menu, sign, notice, package label, or other readable text. You can also pick a photo already on this iPhone. Live translation can appear automatically. An optional shutter freezes the current scene for stable reading, saving, and sharing. When prices are visible, the app may convert them into the iPhone language and currency and overlay that amount on the original price. Overlay amounts do not show the word “approx.” Converted prices are approximate, using that day’s published rate, and are not a bank, card, or restaurant charge.

The app assists understanding only. It is not a certified translation, medical, allergy-safety, dietary, religious, or billing service.

3. How processing is split

  • On-device Apple Vision OCR reads visible text.
  • Deterministic code parses prices/currency when present, counts free uses, and manages the 30-Day Travel Pass from verified StoreKit transactions. Price patterns are a menu signal, not a requirement for signs or general text.
  • On-device AI (Apple Foundation Models when available) may explain the visible scene. If AI is unavailable, OCR + translation + price still work.
  • Apple Translation may translate recognized text into the iPhone language. Depending on the country and which language packs are already on the iPhone, Apple may ask the user to download translation data the first time a pair is used. Download, cancel, or failure does not consume a free read.

AI output can be wrong or incomplete. Do not treat it as a safety or allergen judgment. Numeric prices, entitlement, expiry, and free-use counts are never taken from the AI.

4. Images and text

Live camera frames and user-selected photos are processed on the device. The app is not designed to upload scene images or recognized text to NEXTBASE, OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, or any NextBase AI server. If you choose Save, the composed image can be written to your Photo Library. If you choose Share, the system share sheet stays under your control.

5. Free allowance

This installation includes 3 successful reads of new scenes. The free allowance does not renew each calendar month. A use is consumed only when a new fingerprint produces a valid result. Repeating the same scene does not consume another use. Empty / no-text scenes, cancelled purchases, and analysis failures do not consume a use. When a Travel Pass is active, free uses are not consumed.
