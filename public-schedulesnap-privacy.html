Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Event Sniper processes schedule photos, extracted text, calendar writes, sharing, and purchase status.

Effective: August 27, 2026 · Last updated: September 5, 2026

1. Operator

Event Sniper is operated by Norimasa Nakamura, trading as NEXT BASE-Japan ("NEXTBASE," "we," "us," or "our"). Privacy questions may be sent to info@nextbase-japan.com.

2. Purpose of the app

Event Sniper is a productivity utility that lets a user photograph a paper schedule, whiteboard, or screenshot, or choose an existing photo, so the app can propose calendar-event candidates on the iPhone. The user reviews the candidates and confirms a single batch add. Nothing is written to Calendar until that confirmation.

Event Sniper assists with reading a schedule on the device. It does not guarantee complete extraction, correct dates, or that every event on a page will be found.

3. Camera and selected photos

Camera permission is requested so the user can photograph a schedule. The user may also choose an existing photo through Apple’s system photo picker. Event Sniper is not designed to browse unrelated Photo Library content or upload schedule photos to a NEXTBASE server.

4. On-device reading

Text recognition and schedule interpretation are designed to use Apple’s Vision framework and on-device language assistance on the user’s iPhone, together with deterministic date and time checks. Event Sniper is not designed to send schedule photos or extracted text to NEXTBASE or to an external AI provider.

5. Calendar writes

Calendar access is requested as write-only so confirmed events can be added to the user’s default calendar. Existing calendar events are not read. A silent automatic write does not occur. The user remains responsible for reviewing titles, dates, times, locations, and reminders before confirming.

6. Sharing

If the user taps Share, iOS Share Sheet sends only the text summary and ICS file the user chooses to a destination the user selects. NEXTBASE does not control what that destination does after sharing.

7. No NEXTBASE account or location

The initial version does not require a NEXTBASE account or login. It is not designed to request precise location, access contacts, or maintain a server-side schedule history.

8. Purchases and StoreKit

Three successful schedule imports are available each calendar month without a subscription. One confirmed Calendar save of a photographed or selected schedule counts as one import, even when that save adds several events. Unused free imports do not roll into the next month.

Monthly Unlimited is an auto-renewable subscription processed by Apple through StoreKit and the user’s Apple Account. The exact localized price shown by Apple governs. NEXTBASE does not receive full payment-card details. The app may receive product identifiers, transaction identifiers, and entitlement status needed to provide or restore Premium access.

9. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The initial version is not designed to include advertising, cross-app advertising tracking, an external analytics SDK, or sale of personal information. Apple may provide aggregated or diagnostic information according to the user’s device settings and Apple’s privacy practices.

10. Support communications

If the user emails support, NEXTBASE may receive the email address, message, device and app details voluntarily provided, and attachments included by the user. Users should not send private unedited photos of other people’s schedules, identity documents, children’s information, payment information, health information, or confidential material unless strictly necessary and lawful.

11. Retention and deletion

NEXTBASE does not intentionally retain schedule photos or extracted text on a NEXTBASE server through the core workflow. Local app data may be deleted through iOS storage controls or app deletion. Events written to Calendar remain under the user’s control in the Calendar app.

12. Children

Event Sniper is not directed to children below the minimum age at which they may independently consent to data processing in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children through the core app workflow.
